The Clarity
Sessions.

Foundational coaching and advisory to excavate your core narrative, finding clear, resonant language for your work and its audience.

Whether in tech, NGOs or the art world, complex work often relies on insider language that fails to resonate with an outside audience and clouds things internally, too. Particularly important for individuals and teams in their early stages or in transition, these sessions offer an external eye that gets to the core of what you do, finding language that resonates with you and the audience you want to reach. You leave the sessions with a clear basis not only for external communications, whether pitch decks, web, or gallery texts, but internal cohesion, from product to hiring and more. Because you can’t grow what you don’t see.

Together we will:

  • ✦Diagnose the underlying pattern in your work
  • ✦Extract its core theme and conceptual architecture
  • ✦Identify what you’re really trying to say
  • ✦Translate it into clear language that resonates with your audience

While the primary value lies in the sessions themselves, you also leave with a working document, a distilled reference for your narrative, voice, and positioning that you can use internally and hand to anyone working on your communications.

Who They’re For

  • For startup founders getting clear on what they’re building, and for whom.
  • For artists finding the vocabulary to talk about their work.
  • For NGOs & foundations whose mission has outgrown its language.
  • For anyone seeking sharper messaging, clearer positioning, and a stronger sense of what they stand for.

Format · 3–5 sessions
